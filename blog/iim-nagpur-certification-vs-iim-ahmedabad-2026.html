When IIM Nagpur Blended MBA Is Better

Choose IIM Nagpur when: you specifically need an MBA degree (not a certificate) — for roles with degree eligibility requirement, international applications requiring a degree, or when 'MBA degree from IIM' is specifically the credential you're building toward. IIM Nagpur is the only IIM that gives a full MBA degree to working professionals.

When IIM Ahmedabad AGMP Is Better

Choose IIM Ahmedabad when: your primary goal is the strongest management brand in India; you want to complete in 1 year; you have 5+ years experience; the ₹8L fee is manageable; and you need general management positioning rather than the MBA degree specifically. IIMA alumni status carries the highest recognition across all industries and functions.

"IIM Nagpur MBA gives you a degree. IIM Ahmedabad AGMP gives you India's #1 management brand. If you need the degree, go Nagpur. If you need the brand, go Ahmedabad."

Frequently Asked Questions

Is IIM Nagpur or IIM Ahmedabad certification better?

Depends on your goal. IIM Ahmedabad AGMP (₹8L, 1 year) gives India's most recognised management brand — stronger for general management, C-suite and multi-industry credibility. IIM Nagpur Blended MBA (₹12.10L, 2 years) gives a full MBA degree — uniquely valuable when the 'MBA degree' designation is specifically required. Different use cases.

Which IIM has more value — Nagpur or Ahmedabad?

IIM Ahmedabad has significantly higher brand recognition nationally — it is ranked #1 by NIRF for 6 consecutive years, triple crown accredited. IIM Nagpur (NIRF #25) is newer but a legitimate central government IIM. For most career purposes, IIMA credentials carry more immediate weight. The IIM Nagpur MBA degree advantage is specifically when the degree designation matters over the certificate.

What is the fee difference between IIM Nagpur and IIM Ahmedabad?

IIM Nagpur Blended MBA: ₹12,10,000 (2-year MBA degree). IIM Ahmedabad AGMP: ₹8,00,000 (1-year certificate). IIM Ahmedabad is actually cheaper by ₹4.10L and takes half the time — but gives a certificate, not a degree.
