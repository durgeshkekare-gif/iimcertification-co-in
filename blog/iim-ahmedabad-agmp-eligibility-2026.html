IIM Ahmedabad AGMP Eligibility 2026 — Who Can Apply & Selection Process

August 12, 2026 · 6 min read · iimcertification.co.in

IIM Ahmedabad AGMP — Eligibility at a Glance

AGMP Eligibility: Graduate with minimum 50% marks + minimum 5 years of post-qualification work experience. No CAT/GMAT required. Selection is based on application, work experience quality, and a selection process conducted by IIM Ahmedabad.

Detailed Eligibility Criteria

How IIM Ahmedabad Selects Candidates for AGMP

IIM-A does not use a standardised entrance test for AGMP. Selection is holistic, based on: quality and progression of work experience (5+ years, senior trajectory), academic background, statement of purpose (why AGMP, career goals), and short-listing interview conducted by IIM Ahmedabad faculty. Candidates with clear senior management career goals and progressive work experience have the strongest profiles.

Ideal Candidate Profile for AGMP

  • 5-10 years of work experience with clear career progression
  • Current role at senior executive, manager, or department head level
  • Targeting VP, Director, C-suite, or general management roles
  • Company sponsored or self-funded — both common
  • Industries: IT, BFSI, consulting, manufacturing, FMCG, healthcare, government

AGMP Cohort — What the Batch Looks Like

AGMP batch size is approximately 50-60 participants per cohort. The batch is intentionally curated for diversity: a mix of industries, functions, and geographies is sought by IIM Ahmedabad. This diversity of peer cohort is a key value of the programme — participants learn as much from each other as from faculty.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the work experience requirement for IIM Ahmedabad AGMP?

IIM Ahmedabad AGMP requires minimum 5 years of post-qualification work experience. This is a firm eligibility criterion. Candidates with fewer than 5 years are not eligible. The programme is designed for mid-to-senior professionals and the curriculum assumes this experience level.

Is GMAT or CAT required for IIM Ahmedabad AGMP?

No — AGMP does not require CAT or GMAT scores. Selection is based on work experience quality, academic background, statement of purpose, and an IIM Ahmedabad selection process. This makes AGMP accessible to professionals who are not test-takers.

How competitive is AGMP admission?

AGMP is selective — IIM Ahmedabad curates each cohort for experience diversity and career seriousness. Candidates with clear career progression, senior-role experience, and a specific rationale for pursuing AGMP (rather than vague 'leadership development' goals) have stronger applications. Having a company-backed application adds credibility but is not mandatory.
