August 05, 2026 · 6 min read · iimcertification.co.in

Why Supply Chain Leaders Need IIM Credentials

Operations and supply chain has historically been undervalued in Indian corporate hierarchies — finance and marketing heads have occupied boardroom seats while supply chain directors were seen as execution specialists. Post-2020, this has changed dramatically. Supply chain resilience is now a board priority. The leaders who will occupy COO and board-level supply chain roles are those who combine operational expertise with management credentials.

IIM Kozhikode Supply Chain & Analytics — Best Specialist Option

IIM Kozhikode's Advanced Supply Chain Management & Role of Analytics is the most targeted IIM programme for supply chain professionals. 6 months, ~₹1.75L, online with IIM-K campus. EQUIS + AMBA accredited. Covers: demand forecasting with analytics, inventory optimisation models, logistics network design, digital supply chain (IoT, AI), and supply chain risk management.

Frequently Asked Questions

Which IIM is best for supply chain management in India 2026?

IIM Kozhikode's Advanced Supply Chain Management & Analytics (~₹1.75L, 6 months) is the most specifically targeted IIM programme for supply chain professionals. It combines supply chain strategy with analytics application. IIM Trichy's programmes are better for broader general management including operations management.

What certifications are useful for supply chain professionals in India?

Beyond IIM programmes: APICS CPIM (Certified in Planning and Inventory Management), APICS CSCP (Certified Supply Chain Professional), SCPro (Council of Supply Chain Management Professionals), Six Sigma Green/Black Belt, and Lean Management certification. The combination of an IIM certificate + CPIM is particularly strong for senior supply chain leadership.

Can supply chain engineers do IIM programmes?

Yes — IIM executive programmes are open to working professionals from all backgrounds including engineering. Supply chain engineers with 3+ years experience are eligible for IIM Kozhikode Supply Chain Analytics, IIM Nagpur DS for Business, and IIM Trichy eGMP. No management qualification required for entry.
