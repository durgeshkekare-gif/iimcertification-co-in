This is wrong. Several IIM programmes specifically target operations, supply chain, and manufacturing domains — with curriculum directly applicable to factory floors, SCM strategy, and industrial operations.

IIM Kozhikode Supply Chain & Analytics — Deep Dive

The Advanced Supply Chain Management & Analytics programme from IIM Kozhikode is specifically designed for supply chain, logistics, and operations professionals. Curriculum: supply chain strategy, demand forecasting, inventory optimisation, warehouse management, analytics for SCM, ESG in supply chain, and global trade. No coding required — business analytics approach. Fee: ~₹1,75,000 + GST, 6 months, IIM-K alumni status.

Lean Six Sigma + IIM Certificate — Powerful Combination

Lean Six Sigma Black Belt (LSSBB) is the gold standard technical credential for operations professionals. Combined with an IIM management certificate, it creates the most competitive operations leadership profile: technical process expertise + IIM management credibility + strategic frameworks. Operations professionals with LSSBB + IIM credential command 30-50% salary premium over technical-only peers at Director/VP level.

Frequently Asked Questions

Which IIM programme is best for supply chain professionals?

IIM Kozhikode Advanced Supply Chain Management & Analytics (~₹1,75,000, 6 months) is the most directly relevant IIM programme for supply chain professionals. It covers SCM strategy, analytics, and global trade with no coding requirement. For senior SCM heads (10+ years): IIM Kozhikode NAMP (₹4L) for broader leadership frameworks.

Can operations managers do IIM courses?

Yes — several IIM programmes are specifically suited for operations and manufacturing professionals. IIM Kozhikode Supply Chain & Analytics, IIM Trichy SMP, and NAMP are all accessible to operations professionals. Most require no functional background restriction — just minimum work experience (3-10 years depending on programme).

What salary hike can operations professionals expect after IIM certificate?

Operations professionals with IIM certificate typically see 25-45% salary increase when actively using the credential to change roles or get promoted. The highest impact comes from using the IIM credential to move from technical operations management into general management or P&L ownership roles at 8-12 years experience.
