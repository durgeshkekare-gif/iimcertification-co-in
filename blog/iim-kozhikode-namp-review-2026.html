IIM Kozhikode NAMP Review 2026 — Is It Worth Rs 4 Lakh for Senior Professionals?

August 27, 2026 · 7 min read · iimcertification.co.in

IIM Kozhikode NAMP — What It Is

The New Age Management Programme for Senior Professionals (NAMP) from IIM Kozhikode is a 10-month executive certificate for working professionals with minimum 10 years of experience. Fee: Rs 4,00,000 + GST (~Rs 4,72,000 all-in). Online with campus modules at IIM Kozhikode. IIM Kozhikode is NIRF #3 in Management, EQUIS and AMBA dual accredited — the third-strongest IIM brand after IIM Ahmedabad and IIM Bangalore.

NAMP Curriculum — What You Learn

  • Strategy and competitive dynamics — business model innovation, strategic frameworks for disruption
  • Leadership and organisational transformation — leading change, culture, talent strategy
  • Financial management — for non-finance senior managers and P&L owners
  • Digital and AI strategy — technology decisions at board level, not implementation
  • Marketing for senior leaders — brand equity, customer experience strategy
  • Capstone project — applying all frameworks to your actual organisation

The 10-Year Experience Requirement — Why It Matters

NAMP is genuinely not designed for early or mid-career professionals. The curriculum assumes you are already running teams, owning P&Ls, and making strategic decisions. The case discussions are peer-to-peer among experienced professionals — the learning model is cohort-driven. At less than 10 years experience, you would be in the wrong cohort for the right learning.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is IIM Kozhikode NAMP worth it?

Yes — for senior professionals with 10+ years experience targeting C-suite. IIM Kozhikode is India's #3 management school (NIRF) with EQUIS+AMBA dual accreditation. At Rs 4.72L all-in for 10 months, it is significantly better value than IIM Ahmedabad AGMP (Rs 9.44L) for professionals who primarily need IIM brand and management frameworks rather than IIM-A specifically.

What experience is needed for IIM Kozhikode NAMP?

Minimum 10 years of post-qualification full-time work experience. This is a firm requirement — not a guideline. The programme is specifically designed for senior professionals and the curriculum assumes this experience level. Candidates with less than 10 years should consider IIM Ahmedabad AGMP (5 years minimum) or IIM Nagpur Blended MBA (3 years minimum).

Does NAMP give IIM Kozhikode alumni status?

Yes — completing NAMP gives IIM Kozhikode alumni status (lifetime). IIM Kozhikode is NIRF #3 — the alumni network includes CXOs across Indian industry. Alumni events, LinkedIn groups and the IIM-K alumni association are active.
