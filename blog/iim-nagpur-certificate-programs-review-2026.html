Blended MBA vs PGCGM — which IIM Nagpur programme to choose

Choose Blended MBA if: you specifically need a full UGC MBA degree (for government jobs, further study, UPSC, WES), you have CAT/GMAT scores, and can invest ₹3.5–4.5L over 2 years. This is the only full MBA degree programme from an IIM for working professionals in India.

Choose PGCGM if: you want a 1-year IIM certificate without CAT/GMAT, you need faster credentialing, or your budget is ₹1.5–2L. The PGCGM gives you the IIM Nagpur certificate in half the time and at roughly half the cost of the Blended MBA.
